Versa Gripps are a self-supporting grip aid for weightlifting that wraps around the wrist and bar to enhance grip, reduce strain, and provide hand protection. They are made of a proprietary non-slip material and are designed to replace traditional straps, hooks, and gloves. Key features include a built-in arch support for the palm, a safety quick-release feature, and a supportive wrist strap. 
Key features and function
Grip enhancement: The proprietary non-slip material creates a secure grip on barbells and dumbbells, helping lifters handle heavier weights.
Self-supporting design: The grips lock onto the bar with minimal effort from the lifter, and a simple unclasping of the hands releases the bar instantly for safety.
Wrist support: They provide a wrist strap with built-in arch support to help pad and support the palm, which can be especially beneficial during pressing exercises.
Palm protection: The gripping surface is designed to protect the hand from the knurling on weight bars and prevent calluses.
Quick-release safety: Unlike traditional straps, they have a quick-release function that prevents the lifter from being tied to the bar, which can be a safety hazard.
